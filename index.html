Research Summary / 研究概要

[English] This portal hosts the primary manuscripts for the "Wave Refraction Model (WRM)." The theory proposes that galactic rotation curves and cosmic redshift are products of spacetime refractive gradients within a 1-trillion-light-year wave structure, rather than dark matter or physical expansion.
